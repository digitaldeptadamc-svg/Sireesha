Department of Dravyaguna
Sireesha
Botanical Name :

  • Albizia lebbeck (L.) Benth

    • Family Name :

  • Fabaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Śirīṣa

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Siris

  • English – Lebbeck Tree, Woman's Tongue Tree

    • Synonyms :

  • Śirīṣa

  • Śirīṣaka


    • Habitat :

  • Found throughout India in tropical and subtropical regions, commonly grows along roadsides, in forests, gardens and open areas, grows well in warm climates and a variety of soils.


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Large deciduous tree with greyish-brown bark

  • Leaves: Bipinnate, with numerous small leaflets

  • Flowers: Greenish-white to yellowish, fragrant, with numerous long stamens forming fluffy spherical heads


    • Useful Parts :

  • Seeds


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa – Kashaya, Tikta

  • Guna – Laghu, Ruksha

  • Vipaka - Ushna

  • Virya - Katu


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Vishaghna

  • Shothahara

  • Krimighna


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Visha (Poisoning): Śirīṣa is traditionally regarded as an important Vishaghna drug

  • Kushta (Skin Disorders): Bark and seed preparations are traditionally used


    • Dose

  • Bark Churna: 3–6 g

  • Seed Churna: 1–3 g

  • Kwatha: 50–100 ml


    • Formulation:

  • Śirīṣatvak Churna

  • Śirīṣa Agada

  • Ekal Dravya Prayoga
